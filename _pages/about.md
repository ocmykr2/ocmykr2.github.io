I am a PhD student in Computer Science at New York University, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Anupam Gupta](https://cs.nyu.edu/~anupamg/). Prior to that, I received my Bachelor's Degree from the ACM Honors Class at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I am currently exploring research directions in large language models, with particular interests in AI alignment and AI for mathematics. My research background is in theoretical computer science, especially online algorithms, scheduling, and graph algorithms.

I loved competing in programming contests. As captain of Willow, I represented Shanghai Jiao Tong University at the 2023 ICPC World Finals in Luxor. I also won the 2023 ICPC Hong Kong Regional and earned 17 gold medals across ICPC and CCPC competitions. Those medals include gold at the ICPC East Asia Finals in 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2023, as well as at the 2022, 2023 CCPC Final. What I enjoyed most was how smoothly we worked together as a team.
