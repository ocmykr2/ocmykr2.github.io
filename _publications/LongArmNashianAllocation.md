**Authors:** Zhiyi Huang, Chui Shan Lee, Xinkai Shu, and Zhaozi Wang

**Venue:** 52nd International Colloquium on Automata, Languages, and Programming (ICALP 2025), LIPIcs 334, Article 98

**Links:** [Proceedings](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2025.98) | [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13430) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2025.98)

We characterize the optimal competitive ratios for online *p*-mean welfare maximization over the full spectrum from egalitarian to utilitarian welfare. For a broad range of *p*, the result builds on greedy Nash-welfare allocation together with auxiliary components that protect agents with low utility.
