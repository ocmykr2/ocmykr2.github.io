**Authors:** Zhaozi Wang, Zhiwei Ying, and Yuhao Zhang

**Venue:** 21st Conference on Web and Internet Economics (WINE 2025)

**Links:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.11195) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-18660-7_37)

We study online makespan minimization when jobs arrive over time. The dynamic-locking technique achieves a competitive ratio strictly below 1.5 for every fixed number of identical machines, improving on the classical LPT guarantee.
