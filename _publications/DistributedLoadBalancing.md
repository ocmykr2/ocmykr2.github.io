**Authors:** Aaron Bernstein, Anupam Gupta, and Zhaozi Wang

**Status:** Manuscript, arXiv:2607.10075

**Links:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10075) | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.10075)

We give polylog-round algorithms in the CONGEST model for load balancing on unrelated machines, obtaining a $(1+\varepsilon)$-approximate fractional solution and a $(2+\varepsilon)$-approximate integral solution. The result also yields a general polylog-round $(1+\varepsilon)$-approximation for mixed packing-covering linear programs in CONGEST.
